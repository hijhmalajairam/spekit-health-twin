# Health Twin AI – Outreach Plan for Geographical Expansion and User Onboarding

## Objective

Expand Health Twin AI's reach across India by partnering with educational institutions, healthcare providers, and wellness organizations while ensuring a seamless onboarding experience for new users.

## Phase 1 – Local Launch

- Deploy the platform for pilot users in Hyderabad.
- Conduct demonstrations in colleges and healthcare events.
- Collect feedback from early adopters to improve usability.

## Phase 2 – Regional Expansion

- Expand to educational institutions and healthcare organizations across Telangana and neighboring states.
- Collaborate with wellness communities and student innovation programs.
- Promote the platform through hackathons, workshops, and social media campaigns.

## Phase 3 – Nationwide Expansion

- Introduce multilingual support to improve accessibility across different regions of India.
- Partner with hospitals, NGOs, and preventive healthcare initiatives.
- Integrate wearable devices and Electronic Health Records (EHR) to increase adoption.

## User Onboarding Strategy

- Simple user registration and profile creation.
- Guided health assessment through the Health Simulator.
- Interactive tutorials for Body Map, Brain Games, and What-If Simulation.
- AI-generated health insights to encourage continued engagement.
- Continuous feature updates based on user feedback.

## Future Vision

Expand internationally by supporting additional languages, complying with regional healthcare standards, and integrating with global digital health ecosystems.
